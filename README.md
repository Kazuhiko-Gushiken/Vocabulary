# Vocabulary
<hr/>

Juxtapose - Place or deal with close togehter for constrasting effect.

Succinct - (especially of something written or spoken) briefly and clearly expressed.

Encapsulate - Express the essential features of (something) succinctly.

Dichotomy - A division or contrast between two things that are or are represented as being opposed or entirely different.

Star-crossed - (of a person or plan) thwarted by bad luck.

Thwart - prevent (someone or something (plan)) from accomplishing something.

Vicarious - Experienced in the imagination through the feelings of actions of another person.

Adolescence - The period following the onset of puberty during which a young person develops from a child into an adult.

Melancholy - A feeling of pensive sadness, typically with no obvious cause.

Pensive - Engaged in, involving, or reflecting deep or serious thought.

Preordained - (of an outcome or course of action) decided or determed beforehand; predestined.

Compel - Force or oblige (someone) to do something. 

Evoke - Bring or recall to the conscious mind.

Oblige - Make (someone) legally or morally bound to an action or course of action. 

Narrative - A spoken or written account of connected events; a story.

Arbitrary - Based on random choice or personal whim, rather than any reason or system; (of a constant or other quantity) of unspecified value.

Ronin - A Japanese student who has failed a college entrance examination and is studying to take it again.
